BillEasy: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026
Effective date: 2 November 2026

BillEasy ("the App") is operated by Tejasvi Choudhary, Hanumangarh, Rajasthan, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

BillEasy does not collect your personal information on any server of its own. The information you enter in the App, such as shop name, address, GSTIN, bank details, customer and item details, bills, and settings, is stored only on your own device.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

All data stays in the browser on your phone or computer. If you choose a folder for automatic backup, the backup file is saved only in the folder you select.

3. Third-Party Services

Some features of the App use the following external services:

  • QR code: The UPI payment link is sent to api.qrserver.com so that a QR code can be generated. Your bill or customer's personal data is not sent.
  • Excel feature: The Excel library is loaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net.
  • Share and WhatsApp: You send the bill summary yourself, through the app you choose (such as WhatsApp).

4. PIN and Security

If you turn on the PIN, the PIN itself is not saved. Only a hashed (encoded) version of it is saved. Keep your PIN and backup file in a safe place. If you clear the browser's site data, all App data may be deleted.

5. Legal Responsibility

The shop owner is responsible for the customer data they enter, for keeping it secure, and for how it is used. Business users in India should also follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) in their own business practices.

6. Children

The App is intended for business use and is not meant for anyone under 18 years of age.

7. Your Rights

You can view your data in the App at any time, take a backup, delete bills or items, or clear the browser's site data to erase all data.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the new date will be shown at the top.
